# EDR Lab - TryHackMe

## What it was
This was a Tryhackme generated EDR dashboard showing endpoint alerts of different devices on a network.

## The task
The room had a series of questions such as identifying the absolute file path of a malicious command line prompt downloaded by the user.

## What I learned
EDR's are in a sense similar to a SIEM with a dashboard and alerts but where they come from is different. I also learned the importance of having both SIEM and EDR tools because SIEM can not reach endpoint and systems and stop processes on a singular device which the EDR can. 
## What was hard
Because the questions were guided and built off one another, it was manageable even though i had never seen an EDR before. Without the guidance the fake UI can still be quite complex.
